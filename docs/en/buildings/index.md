# Buildings

Buildings unlock cooking, animals, compost, the greenhouse, and more. This page lists every building with its costs.

## Getting started

### Build a building

1. Click the **Workbench**
   <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/workbench.png" alt="Workbench" width="40" height="40" />
   on your farm.
2. Open the **Build** tab to see buildings available for your bumpkin level.
3. Pay the **coins**
   <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/coins.webp" alt="Coins" width="24" height="24" />
   and required **resources**. The building goes into your inventory / chest.

### Place a building

1. Click the **hammer**
   <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/landscaping-hammer.png" alt="Landscaping mode" width="24" height="24" />
   at the bottom of the screen to enter **landscaping** mode.
2. Open the **chest**
   <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/chest.png" alt="Chest" width="24" height="24" />
   (or the quick panel) and pick the **buildings** category
   <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/hammer.png" alt="Buildings" width="24" height="24" />.
3. Select the building, place it on a free spot, then confirm with
   <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/confirm.png" alt="Confirm" width="24" height="24" />
   (or cancel with
   <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/cancel.png" alt="Cancel" width="24" height="24" />
   ).

### Move a building

1. Enter **landscaping** mode with the **hammer**
   <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/landscaping-hammer.png" alt="Landscaping mode" width="24" height="24" />.
2. Click an existing building, then **drag** it
   <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/drag.png" alt="Move" width="24" height="24" />
   to the new spot.
3. Confirm placement with
   <img class="item-icon" src="/icons/confirm.png" alt="Confirm" width="24" height="24" />.

### Upgrade a building

Some buildings have **multiple levels** (Hen House, Barn, Water Well, Pet House, Aging Shed):

1. Click the building already on your farm.
2. Open **Upgrade** if available.
3. Pay the **coins** and resources for the next level (see each card below).

::: tip
Some starter buildings (Betty’s Market, Town Center…) are already on the farm. Others need the bumpkin level below, or a specific island (e.g. Desert for the Greenhouse).

**Homes** (Town Center → House → Manor → Mansion): **not craftable**. They are **granted / replaced** when you upgrade islands.

**Appearance (skins):** many buildings change look by **island / biome** and sometimes by **season**. Cosmetic only (except homes, where the building itself changes).
:::
